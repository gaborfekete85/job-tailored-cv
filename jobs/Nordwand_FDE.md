## Job description
The Nordwand Group is currently looking for a FDE, Forward Deployed Engineer to join their client in central Zurich. The team is small but secure and we want engineers who are passionate about leveraging AI tools to solve real business problems and implement solutions to be apart of their future.
The client is rebuilding core tooling from the ground up—replacing vendor platforms with in-house built solutions designed around how the people work. The teams across the firm use AI tools to rapidly prototype requirements and designs, using them as blueprints for highly engineered, production-ready systems.
As an FDE you will have the following responsibilities:
Work with analysts and architects to understand problems and turn requirements into working prototypes
Use AI tools to prototype requirements and UI designs, and use the results as input for implementation
Design, build, and test production-quality full stack applications
Review AI-generated code for quality, security, and maintainability
Deploy and run applications on Kubernetes

This position suits someone who can distinguish rapid experimentation from production engineering, someone who is excited by AI-assisted development and can make critical judgment when working with AI-generated output tools. 
The person we are looking for:
Around 5-10 years of professional software development experience
Java, Spring Boot, Angular, relational databases (data modelling, SQL), AI tools, experience with deploying applications on Kubernetes (containers, Helm, CI/CD)

If you are interested in this position, please apply with your updated CV to Immanuel Munafo at the Nordwand Group.
